Small wedding venue in San Isidro, Lima: Wasi Café, exclusively yours for 20 to 30 guests

Wasi Café blog · San Isidro, Lima · Updated October 29, 2026

Getting married in Lima with just your closest people? Wasi Café, a specialty coffee shop in San Isidro, Lima's business district, offers a small wedding package: the whole venue exclusively for you, for 20 to 30 guests, with decoration, food and a wedding cake.

The price depends on the date, the number of guests and the menu, so we quote it on WhatsApp.

What the package includes

  • The venue, exclusively yours during the celebration: no other customers.
  • 20 to 30 guests.
  • Decoration for the occasion.
  • Food for your guests, planned with you when we quote.
  • A wedding cake.

Any day of the week, depending on availability.

Who it is for

  • Couples having a civil ceremony in Lima who want a lunch or a toast afterwards nearby.
  • Couples living abroad who come to Lima to marry with their closest family.
  • Intimate weddings where everyone knows each other.

San Isidro is central and easy to reach, and many international hotels are in the district.

The food and the cake

Everything comes from our own kitchen and pastry counter. We plan the menu with you; as an idea of what we make every day: Peruvian sandwiches like pan con chicharrón and butifarra, club sandwiches and filled croissants, fresh juices, chicha morada, Peruvian specialty coffee from Cusco, Cajamarca, Junín, Amazonas and San Martín, and pisco sour for the toast.

For the cake, our pastry counter makes chocolate cake, tres leches, carrot cake and berry cheesecake; we will tell you the options when we quote. See everything with photos in all products.

Why a café for a small wedding

A large banquet hall can feel empty with 25 guests. A café is the opposite: warm, close, made for conversation. With the venue closed to the public, your guests can move around, talk to everyone and enjoy the food without a stiff programme. It also keeps things simple: one place for the toast, the meal and the cake, in a central district that is easy to reach from anywhere in Lima.

Tips for a small wedding in Lima

  • Close the guest list first. At 30 guests, every name matters.
  • Book early if your date matters, especially around long weekends and holidays.
  • Plan the timing between the civil ceremony and the celebration, and allow for Lima traffic.
  • Keep one central moment: a toast and a few words work better than a long programme at an intimate wedding.
  • Photographer or music? Tell us when you ask for a quote so we can plan the space.

How to get a quote

Message us on WhatsApp at +51 908 724 496 with your date and time, the number of guests (20 to 30), the moment (lunch, afternoon toast or dinner), any decoration ideas, and dietary needs. We will send you a proposal with the price.
